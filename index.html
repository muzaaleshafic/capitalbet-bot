BACKUPS & SUPPORT:

Getting Started & Setup: Enter your official Branch Name, select your Tracking Month and Year from the dropdowns, and click "Confirm & lock setup" to initialize your monthly target sheet. Once locked, use the "Edit month" button to switch months or start a new tracking cycle without losing historical records.

Setting Up Targets: Enter your expected target values for Normal Sales, Virtual Profits, Aviator Profits, and Slot Profits. Click "Save" on each row to lock your targets. If you need to modify a locked target later, click Edit and enter the required password permit.

Entering Daily Sales & Profits: The daily sales table automatically generates rows for every day of the selected month. Type your figures into any blank field and press Enter to instantly save and lock that entry. Use a minus sign (e.g., -50000) for losses—negative amounts increase the remaining target balance, while positive amounts reduce it.

📖 How to Use the Branch Target Sheet

Follow this 4-step workflow to configure your branch targets, record daily profits, and monitor performance.

Step 1
1. SETUP BRANCH & MONTH

Enter your Branch Name, select the Tracking Month and Year, then click "Confirm & lock setup". This initializes the monthly tracking sheet and locks the branch context.

Step 2
2. INPUT TARGET BALANCES

Enter expected target figures for NORMAL TARGETS, VIRTUAL TARGETS, AVIATOR TARGETS, and SLOT TARGETS. Click "Save" on each row to lock the target amount.

Step 3
3. ENTER DAILY SALES DATA

Fill in daily figure records row by row for Normal Sales, Virtual Profits, Aviator Profits, and Slot Profits. Press Enter in any cell or click "Edit" / "Save" on the row to lock entries.

Step 4
4. SAVE & MONITOR PROGRESS

Click "Save All Data" at the bottom of the table to save pending entries. Watch the progress bars in Progress Towards Targets and check Remaining to Target calculations update automatically in real-time.
